# 🎨 UI Contrast Improvement - Completed

I've softened the UI contrast for a more comfortable viewing experience. The design now has better balance and reduced eye strain.

---

## ✨ What Changed

### Color Palette Update

**Before (High Contrast):**
- Heading text: Pure Black (#000000)
- Body text: Dark gray (#44474d)  
- Background: Pure white (#ffffff)
- Accent: Bright blue (#0072F5)

**After (Softer & More Comfortable):**
- Heading text: Dark gray (#1F2937) - softer on the eyes
- Body text: Medium gray (#4B5563) - more readable
- Background: Off-white (#FAFBFC) - less harsh
- Accent: Balanced blue (#3B82F6) - more pleasant
- Secondary: Purple accent (#8B5CF6) - softer
